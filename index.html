About

Christopher Scott McClure received his Ph.D. in Government from Georgetown University and was a Postdoctoral Fellow in Harvard University’s Program on Constitutional Government. He is the author of Hobbes and the Artifice of Eternity (Cambridge University Press), which examines how Hobbes reimagines mortality, uncertainty, and meaning at the founding of modern political thought.

That same set of questions — how people search for order and significance when the old certainties fall away — carries directly into his work in documentary television and audio fiction. As a producer on FBI True and Never Seen Again (both Paramount+), he has booked stories, conducted field interviews, and story-edited episodes built around real investigations. As the creator of two independent fiction podcasts, Harvey in Hell and the award-winning The Milkman of St. Gaff’s, he writes and produces original narrative work supported by the Canada Council for the Arts.

He also co-hosts The Reading Group, a podcast working through Plato’s dialogues in conversation with fellow political theorists.
