Political Theorist • Writer • Producer

Christopher Scott McClure

I am a political theorist specializing in the history of political philosophy. My scholarship explores mortality, uncertainty, religion, and existentialism in the history of political thought, particularly in Hobbes and Nietzsche. Those same questions animate my fiction podcasts and documentary television work, where stories become another way of examining how human beings search for meaning in the modern world.

About

Christopher Scott McClure received his Ph.D. in Government from Georgetown University and was a Postdoctoral Fellow in Harvard University's Program on Constitutional Government. He is the author of Hobbes and the Artifice of Eternity (Cambridge University Press) and creator of award-winning fiction podcasts with more than 900,000 downloads. Alongside his academic work, he has produced documentary television for Paramount+, Discovery+, and CBS.
